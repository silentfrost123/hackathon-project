Trust layer

Money and ideas move only when it's safe.

KYC & KYB checks

Talent verifies with a university or national ID. Companies and investors verify with a trade licence and corporate email.

IP Vault & NDA deal rooms

Inventions get a tamper-evident date stamp. Scouts sign a one-click NDA and pay an option fee before seeing full blueprints.

Mentor-verified milestones

A university incubation mentor checks each stage before any backer money is released.

Three-stage Safe Box escrow

Backer funds are released in three parts — each only after mentor sign-off.

40%Production
