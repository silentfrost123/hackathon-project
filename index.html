How a credit is checked

Four takes. About ten minutes.

  1. TAKE1

    Bring the source, not the export

    The project file, the layered PSD, the RAW photos, the draft with its edits. Exports and screenshots don't count.

  2. TAKE2

    We read it against your CV

    The AI checks the file really is the work you listed. Upload a different project and it says so.

  3. TAKE3

    The locked room

    Full screen. Three questions about decisions only the maker would remember — 100 seconds each, no going back. Paste is off. Leave the screen and it's logged; the third time ends the check.

    1:40Warnings 0/2
  4. TAKE4

    Stamped, with a date

    Pass and the credit gets a gold stamp on your profile. Borderline answers go to a mentor instead of a guess.

Work you do on JISR — paid projects and mentor-reviewed exercises — is stamped automatically. Certificates are checked for edits and confirmed with the issuer when they carry a verification link.
